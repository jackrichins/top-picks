# What I want to see

I'm Jack, a product manager lead on Azure Security key management at Microsoft and a member of Microsoft's Cryptography Board. Before that I spent 16 years as a software engineer on SQL Server. I live in Bothell, Washington. Pick things a thoughtful, curious engineer-turned-PM would be glad he didn't miss.

## How to score
- **9 to 10:** pieces like my favorites list at the bottom. Rare.
- **7 to 8:** worth my time today. This includes **timely news I'd want to know about**, not just deep reads. The favorites set the top of the scale, not the cutoff.
- **Below 7:** fine but skippable.

## My favorite Apple writers: score their real posts 7+
Daring Fireball (Gruber), Six Colors (Snell), 512 Pixels (Hackett), MacStories (Viticci), Hypercritical (Siracusa), Liss is More (Casey Liss), Michael Tsai, Spyglass (Kafka), Pogue's Posts, Basic Apple Guy.
- **Yes (7+):** opinion pieces, analysis, reviews, history, linked-list posts with real commentary, and their coverage of the day's top Apple news.
- **No (1 to 3):** podcast episode announcements and recaps (a post that just summarizes or promotes an episode, like Verge posts under /podcast/), "I was on this show" appearance posts, sponsor and "thanks to X for sponsoring" posts, membership pitches, and bare newsletter or issue announcements.

## Top Apple news: score 7+
New products, OS releases, major features, big company news, and legal or regulatory rulings that change how Apple works. Pick the best single version of each story, and prefer my favorite writers over MacRumors, 9to5Mac, and The Verge.

## Highest priority
- **Cryptography and security engineering:** post-quantum crypto, key management, HSMs, confidential computing, real-world crypto implementations (Signal, passports, Apple security), notable breaches with lessons. Skip routine CVE roundups and vendor marketing.
- **Apple, done thoughtfully:** see the Apple sections above. Skip rumor churn, deal posts, and "how to change this setting" filler unless the tip is genuinely clever.
- **AI and LLMs:** what's actually changing in how software gets built and how people work. Skip hype, funding news, and model leaderboard churn.
- **Big ideas and the good life:** psychology, philosophy, leadership, management, focus and productivity, meaning, habits. Big Think, Cal Newport, Seth Godin, Art of Manliness style essays.

## Also good
- Microsoft and Azure news that matters to a security PM.
- Product management and engineering culture.
- Rivian: software updates, owner tips, service, anything Pacific Northwest.
- Pacific Northwest weather (Cliff Mass), outdoors, hiking, backpacking, snowboarding, adventure stories.
- History: deep dives, WWII and the Pacific War, British history, ancient civilizations.
- Science explainers and hopeful, data-driven progress (Our World in Data, Works in Progress, Fix the News).
- Typography, design, and craftsmanship.
- Whimsy and humor: playful apps, comics, xkcd, Crazy Apple Rumors.
- Faith and religious scholarship: BYU Studies, Scripture Central, BYU Speeches.
- BYU sports and Formula 1: big results and good stories, not every recap.
- Photography: iPhone camera craft, landscape photography.
- Thoughtful commentary on current events and policy, but not daily political churn.

## Rarely or never
- Rumors, leaks, and "what to expect" posts.
- Deals, sales, gift guides, and sponsored posts.
- Product spec roundups and minor app updates.
- Celebrity news and outrage bait.
- The same news story repeated across sites (pick the best version once).
